# God Luck With That

A hybrid incremental strategy game played in the browser.

After a shipwreck, your tribe is stranded on an unknown island. An ancient portal is the only way home, and only the gods can open it. To prove your faith, they send you ten challenges: complete one and you are blessed, fail it and you are punished.

Play it at [god-luck-with-that.web.app](https://god-luck-with-that.web.app).

## Features

- **Colony building**: grow from a camp to a city, unlocking new buildings and resources at each stage.
- **Economy**: assign workers to farms, woodcutters, quarries and mines, and keep food and wood flowing to avoid shortages.
- **Seasons**: warm and cold seasons change food production and wood consumption.
- **Research**: chains of permanent bonuses for production, construction, storage, faith and military.
- **Market**: trade resources for gold at prices that react to what you buy and sell.
- **Military**: train infantry, archers and cavalry, build walls and towers, and fight off raids or pay them off.
- **Challenges of the gods**: build altars, temples and monuments, gather faith and offer tributes. Choose a blessing on success, suffer a punishment on failure.
- **Languages**: English, Spanish, French, Italian and Catalan.

The game is designed for desktop browsers and is not available on touch devices. Progress is saved in the browser (manual save, F9 quick save, or autosave).
